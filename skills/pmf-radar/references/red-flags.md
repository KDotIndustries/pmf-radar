# Red Flags

Use red flags to keep verdicts adversarial. The report is not validation; it is
evidence to decide what to validate next.

## Build Now Red Flags

Do not recommend Build now when:
- pain evidence is mostly weak, stale, or from non-buyers
- no clear buyer or economic owner exists
- there is no evidence of spend, urgent workflow pain, or repeated frequency
- the first wedge cannot be tested in 1-2 weeks
- trust/compliance risk dominates the workflow
- the founder cannot reach the buyer directly

## Run Paid Test Red Flags

Do not recommend Run paid test when:
- the buyer is unclear or cannot approve payment
- outreach channels are speculative
- the offer cannot be expressed as a concrete paid pilot
- the evidence shows curiosity but no willingness to pay
- legal, procurement, privacy, or safety review would block a quick test

## Research More Red Flags

Research more is appropriate when:
- signals are promising but incomplete
- complaints exist but spend is unclear
- buyer and user are different and the economic owner is unknown
- competitors exist but their weakness is not specific
- validation evidence is only a waitlist, detailed interview, or strong reply
  without payment or workflow access

## Park Red Flags

Park is appropriate when:
- the idea is interesting but not urgent
- distribution is weak or too expensive
- the market is crowded without a sharp competitive gap
- the problem is real but infrequent
- buyers may not prioritize the workflow this quarter
- validation would take too long for the founder's current constraints

## Kill Red Flags

Kill is appropriate when:
- pain is weak or mostly invented by the founder
- buyer clarity is missing
- no existing spend or workaround exists
- competitors solve the workflow well enough
- target buyers won't pay for software in this category
- trust/compliance risk makes the wedge unrealistic
- evidence against the idea is stronger than evidence for it

## Internal Tool Only Red Flags

Internal tool only is appropriate when:
- the founder has a real personal workflow but no evidence others share it
- the tool saves time for one team but lacks a repeatable buyer
- the market has low willingness to pay
- the workflow depends on private context that does not generalize
- users might use it if free but would not buy it

## Regulated Markets

For regulated markets such as healthcare, finance, legal, insurance, and elder
care, add specific red flags for:
- patient, customer, client, or elder safety
- privacy and sensitive personal information
- licensing or professional responsibility
- auditability and recordkeeping
- procurement and security review
- liability when AI output is wrong
- inability to use public examples as proof of safe deployment
